Winter 2027

Two weeks in January or February · St. Louis + Port Harcourt

Researched 26 November 2026 · visa rules re-checked 27 November 2026

The short version

  • Japan is the strongest pick for two weeks. Most to do, low daily costs, and winter suits it. The Nigerian passport needs a visa: apply in December for a February trip. Full plan below.
  • Thailand is the best alternative. No visa for the US passport, an online e-visa in about a week for the Nigerian one, low costs, and its best weather of the year. Full plan below.
  • Vietnam is the other easy one: the same $25 online e-visa for both passports, low costs, and a north-to-south route that fills two weeks. Go in mid to late January or after mid-February to miss Tet (around 6 Feb 2027).
  • Barbados is the easiest on visas (none for either passport) but the most expensive, and its only practical flight from Nigeria runs once or twice a month.
  • New: three more easy ones. Senegal needs no visa for either passport and is the shortest flight from Nigeria; Cape Verde adds islands and the Mindelo Carnival (7 to 9 Feb 2027); Malaysia is the cheapest overall and a Thailand alternative. Compare them below.
  • Also costed: Tanzania, Egypt and Italy. Tanzania (safari plus Zanzibar) is the most memorable and the most expensive; Egypt has perfect winter weather and a nonstop flight from Lagos, but an embassy visa for the Nigerian passport; Italy needs the same slow Schengen visa as Germany. Compare them below.
  • Germany is the cheapest of the first three, but the Schengen visa wait makes January unrealistic for the Nigerian passport.
Assumptions

The St. Louis traveller holds a US passport; the Port Harcourt traveller holds a Nigerian passport. Both meet at the destination. Costs are for one 14-night trip, both people combined, one mid-range room. If either passport is different, the visa rows change.

What two weeks looks like

Germany

Berlin (4 nights), Cologne for Carnival around Rose Monday, 8 Feb 2027 (3), the Rhine or Heidelberg (2), Munich and the Alps (5). All by train. Also: Baden-Baden spas, skiing at Garmisch.

Barbados

Two bases: the calm west coast for beaches, catamarans and turtles, then the rugged east coast or the livelier south. Harrison's Cave, rum distilleries, Oistins fish fry, Holetown Festival in mid-February.

Japan

Tokyo (5 nights), Hakone hot springs (2), Kyoto and Nara (4), then Sapporo for the Snow Festival, 4 to 11 Feb 2027 (3). Full plan below.

Japan in detail

Winter is Japan's clearest season: dry air, the best views of Mount Fuji, hot springs at their best, snow in the north, and cheaper hotels than spring. Time the trip to include 4 to 11 February 2027 to catch the Sapporo Snow Festival.

Two-week route

  • Tokyo, 5 nights. Asakusa and Senso-ji, Shibuya Crossing, teamLab, Tsukiji outer market, a sumo stable morning practice, a day trip to Nikko or Kamakura.
  • Hakone, 2 nights. A ryokan with its own hot spring and a kaiseki dinner, the ropeway over the volcanic valley, Lake Ashi with Fuji behind it.
  • Kyoto, 4 nights. Fushimi Inari at sunrise, Kiyomizu-dera, Gion in the evening, Arashiyama bamboo, a day in Nara with the deer. Plum blossoms start late February.
  • Sapporo, 3 nights. Snow sculptures at Odori Park, Susukino ice carvings, a day in Otaru's canal town, or a ski day at Niseko. Fly home via Tokyo.
  • Not going in Snow Festival week? Swap Sapporo for Kanazawa and the snowy thatched village of Shirakawa-go.

Costs for 2, 14 nights

  • Flights: St. Louis about $1,100 to $1,650; Port Harcourt via Lagos about $1,400 to $1,950.
  • Hotels: $80 to $150 a night, $1,120 to $2,100. Sapporo doubles during the festival: book early.
  • Food, metro, sights: $150 to $200 a day, $2,100 to $2,800.
  • Trains, the flight to Sapporo, the Hakone ryokan upgrade: $700 to $1,300.
  • Nigerian visa: ¥15,000 (about $100) plus the VFS fee. Japan's ¥3,000 departure tax is already in airline tickets.
  • Total: about $6,500 to $9,900.

The Nigerian visa

  • Apply at the VFS Japan Visa Application Centre in Lagos or Abuja (closed weekends and Nigerian holidays). At least 12 to 14 working days. Fee ¥15,000 single entry since July 2026 (it was ¥3,000).
  • Bring: passport with 2 blank pages, the signed application form, a photo, a flight itinerary with flight numbers and times, and recent bank statements.
  • A hotel booking is not required to apply.
  • Self-employed or not working: add a self-introduction letter.
  • If the US traveller is paying, include their bank statement too; the embassy asks for the payer's.
  • For an 18 January start, apply by about 19 December; for the Snow Festival, by early January.

Tips

  • Skip the 14-day Japan Rail Pass (¥80,000 each). For this route, single tickets plus one flight cost less.
  • Load a Suica or Pasmo card on your phone for trains, buses and convenience stores.
  • Pack for cold: Tokyo 2 to 10 °C, Sapporo well below freezing. Shoes with grip for icy pavements.
  • Lunar New Year week (around 6 Feb) is busier in Tokyo and Kyoto.
  • Fill in Visit Japan Web before flying for a faster arrival.

Thailand in detail

January and February are Thailand's best months: dry, sunny, and cooler in the north. Use the Andaman coast (Krabi, Koh Lanta) for beaches; it is the drier side in January.

Two-week route

  • Bangkok, 3 nights. Grand Palace and Wat Pho, a long-tail boat on the canals, Chinatown street food, a rooftop bar.
  • Chiang Mai, 4 nights. Old-city temples, a cooking class, an ethical elephant sanctuary, Doi Inthanon (cool, bring a jacket), a day trip to the White Temple in Chiang Rai. One-hour flight from Bangkok.
  • Krabi and Railay, 4 nights. Limestone cliffs, kayaking, the four-islands boat trip. Fly Chiang Mai to Krabi.
  • Koh Lanta, 3 nights. Quiet beaches to finish; ferry from Krabi, then fly home from Krabi via Bangkok.

Costs for 2, 14 nights

  • Flights: St. Louis about $1,000 to $1,350; Port Harcourt via Lagos and Doha about $1,250 to $1,550.
  • Hotels: $50 to $120 a night, $700 to $1,680.
  • Food, local transport, sights: $80 to $150 a day, $1,120 to $2,100.
  • Two domestic flights and ferries: about $200 to $400.
  • Nigerian e-visa: about $30. US passport: free.
  • Total: about $4,300 to $7,100.

Timing and tips

  • Both travellers fill in the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card online within 72 hours before landing.
  • US passport: 30 days visa-free. Nigerian passport: 60-day e-visa from the official portal. Nigerian applicants first need an NDLEA visa clearance and a POSSAP police clearance; then the embassy in Abuja quotes 15 business days, so start six weeks ahead. Document checklist.
  • No tourist entry fee is charged. The 300 baht plan was shelved; a 450 baht fee was discussed in August 2026 with no start date. Budget about $15 each just in case.
  • Chinese New Year (around 6 Feb 2027) fills Bangkok's Chinatown: great to see, book rooms early.
  • Temples need covered shoulders and knees. Grab works for taxis.

Twin-centre ideas

Two countries in one trip, chosen so neither passport picks up a hard visa. Totals are rough, for 2 people, 14 nights.

Thailand + Vietnam

7 nights each: Bangkok and Chiang Mai, then a 2-hour flight to Hanoi, Ha Long Bay and Hoi An. Both are online visas. Fly into Bangkok and home from Da Nang or Ho Chi Minh City. About $4,500 to $7,800. Avoid Vietnam during Tet (around 6 Feb).

Sri Lanka + Maldives

(Only if the Nigerian traveller can get the sponsored Sri Lanka visa.) 10 nights in Sri Lanka, then 4 on a Maldives island, an hour's flight away. The Maldives gives every nationality a free 30-day visa on arrival, and January to March is its dry season. Resorts are pricey: about $6,500 to $11,000.

Kenya safari + the coast

Already the plan above, but for more variety add Lamu island (old Swahili town, dhow trips) for 3 nights instead of more Diani. Still no visa for the Nigerian passport and an eTA for the US one.

Japan + Thailand

Snow and hot springs for 7 nights, then beaches for 7. Bangkok to Tokyo is about 6 hours. Only worth it if the Nigerian Japan visa is in hand; a little under Japan alone, about $5,800 to $9,000.

Vietnam in detail

The one place on this list where both passports use the same quick online visa. Fly into Hanoi and out of Ho Chi Minh City (an "open-jaw" ticket) so you never backtrack.

Two-week route

  • Hanoi, 3 nights. Old Quarter, street food, the Temple of Literature, a water-puppet show.
  • Ha Long Bay, 1 night. Overnight cruise among the limestone islands.
  • Hue, 2 nights. The old imperial city and royal tombs. Fly or take the train south.
  • Hoi An, 3 nights. Lantern-lit old town, tailors, cooking classes, An Bang beach, a day at the Marble Mountains near Da Nang.
  • Ho Chi Minh City, 3 nights. War Remnants Museum, Cu Chi tunnels, a day trip to the Mekong Delta.
  • Phu Quoc, 2 nights. Beach to finish, short flight from Ho Chi Minh City.

Costs for 2, 14 nights

  • Flights: St. Louis about $900 to $1,450; Port Harcourt via Lagos about $1,100 to $1,950 with the domestic hop.
  • Hotels: $40 to $100 a night, $560 to $1,400.
  • Food, local transport, sights: $70 to $130 a day, $980 to $1,820.
  • Domestic flights and the Ha Long cruise: about $400 to $800.
  • E-visas: $50 for both.
  • Total: about $4,000 to $7,500.

Timing and tips

  • Tet Lunar New Year falls around 6 February 2027. Businesses close for several days and transport sells out from late January. Best windows: mid to late January or after about 15 February.
  • Pack layers: Hanoi can be 12 °C and grey while Ho Chi Minh City is 32 °C.
  • Apply for the e-visa only at the official portal. Look-alike sites charge more.
  • Cash (dong) is still common; Grab handles taxis and food delivery.

Senegal

Why: the only place on the list where neither passport needs anything, and the closest to Port Harcourt. French-speaking, with music, food and history (Goree Island).

Route: Dakar 4 nights, Saint-Louis 3, Sine-Saloum delta 3, Saly or Somone beach 4. A car and driver between them is in the cost.

Watch: fewer big-name sights than Asia; beaches are good, not Thai-level.

Cape Verde

Why: Atlantic islands with reliable dry winter weather, morna music and a carnival in the dates. Pairs well with Senegal: meet in Dakar, fly on together.

Route: Praia and Cidade Velha 3 nights, Mindelo 4 (carnival), Santo Antao 2, Sal 5. Inter-island flights and ferries are in the cost.

Watch: few flights, so book early; the Nigerian rule changed in November 2026.

Malaysia

Why: the lowest total here, easy English, superb food, and Thailand's variety with fewer crowds. No visa for the US passport.

Route: Kuala Lumpur 4 nights, Melaka 2, Cameron Highlands 2, Penang 3, Langkawi 3.

Watch: the longest flights for the St. Louis traveller; check the Nigerian eVisa on the official portal.

Tanzania + Zanzibar

Costs for 2: safari $3,000 to $5,800 (5 nights, lodges, park fees, meals); Arusha to Zanzibar flight about $300 to $450; Tanzania's compulsory insurance (covers Zanzibar too) $44 each; e-visas about $150; 9 nights of hotel and food on Zanzibar.

Why: the big-game safari of a lifetime plus a beach week, in the best season for both. A cheaper option than Kenya's Masai Mara only if you pick a budget camping safari.

Watch: the single most expensive choice here; the safari price depends heavily on lodge level and group size.

Egypt

Costs for 2: 4-night Nile cruise $1,300 to $2,100 with guided temples; domestic flights, Abu Simbel and site tickets about $400 to $700; 10 nights of hotel and food.

Why: the pyramids, Luxor's temples and a Nile cruise in perfect weather, with the cheapest, shortest flight from Nigeria of any long-haul choice.

Watch: the Nigerian visa is the whole question: it has to come from the embassy, so apply before booking anything.

Italy

Costs for 2: trains about $250 to $450; Schengen visa and service fees $100 to $200; 14 nights of hotel and food at European prices.

Why: art, food and history everywhere, in the quietest months, with Venice Carnival inside a February trip.

Watch: like Germany, January is unrealistic for the Nigerian visa; for February, book the visa appointment now.

About Air Peace (the Lagos to Barbados airline)
  • Nigeria's largest private airline, founded in 2013 and based in Lagos. It flies across Nigeria (Port Harcourt included), West Africa, and long-haul to places like London, Dubai and the Caribbean.
  • The Caribbean route started on 24 May 2026: Lagos to Antigua, then Barbados, on a Boeing 777. Sources disagree on frequency: monthly, or twice a month on the last two Mondays. No December or January dates were published.
  • It is the only route from Nigeria to the Caribbean that skips US, UK and European connections.
  • Reliability Known for delays and cancellations; Nigeria's aviation regulator has told it to improve. With so few flights, a cancellation could cost days. Leave slack and buy travel insurance.
  • Book at flyairpeace.com or through a Nigerian travel agent.
Connections for the Nigerian passport
  • Avoid routes through the US. Since 1 January 2026 the US restricts new visas for Nigerians, and you can't transit the US without one.
  • Avoid routes through the UK unless the UK transit visa or a same-day exemption applies.
  • Frankfurt (with a Schengen visa), Doha, Dubai, Addis Ababa and Nairobi are the usual hubs. Check airside transit with the airline when booking.
  • Nigerian bank cards often have low limits abroad; bring a dollar or euro card or cash.
Booking checklist (whichever place wins)
  • Pick by mid-December. The Paperwork deadlines page lists the latest safe start for each country's paperwork, for both passports; the Nigerian passport sets the pace everywhere except Barbados and Kenya.
  • Passports: both valid at least 6 months past the return date, with two blank pages.
  • Flights: book so both arrive the same day, ideally within a few hours; the earlier arrival checks in to the first hotel. Avoid connections through the US or UK for the Nigerian passport.
  • Insurance that covers cancellations and missed connections, important if Air Peace or a visa delay is involved.
  • Yellow fever card for the Nigerian traveller: required by Barbados for arrivals from Nigeria, part of the ECOWAS entry rules (Senegal, Cape Verde), recommended for Kenya and Tanzania, and often checked when leaving Nigeria.
  • Money: a dollar or euro card for the Nigerian traveller (naira cards have low limits abroad), and a no-foreign-fee card for the US traveller.
  • Phones: an eSIM for the destination for both, so maps and ride apps work on landing.
  • Arrival forms online in the last 72 hours: Visit Japan Web, Thailand Digital Arrival Card, Barbados travel form, as needed.
