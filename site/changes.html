What's changed

November 26, 2026

  • Numbers checked on every update. The site now refuses to publish if any cost in the tables, route plans or chart stops matching the trip data the planner uses, so all the figures on the site agree.
  • Plan this trip. Every result in the Trip Planner opens into a cost breakdown with each traveller's share (each pays their own flight, the rest is split), a dated list of steps (start paperwork, book flights, festivals and holidays, trip dates), and an Add to calendar button that downloads those dates as an .ics file for Google, Apple or Outlook calendars.
  • Trip Planner has its own page. "Pick for us" moved from the Winter 2027 page to planner.html, with a Planner link in the menu. Each destination name now links to its full plan, and Copy link sends your dates and priorities to the other traveller.
  • Japan added as a full option. A two-week Tokyo, Hakone, Kyoto and Sapporo route timed for the Snow Festival (4 to 11 Feb 2027), the Nigerian visa documents, updated flights, and trains and the ryokan now counted in the cost.
  • Pick for us. An interactive picker on the Winter 2027 page: set dates, nights, hotel level and what matters most, and the seven destinations re-rank with costs for two, the latest safe date to start visas, and holidays or festivals inside your dates.
  • Thailand added as a full option. A two-week Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Krabi and Koh Lanta route, updated flights and costs, the digital arrival card, entry-fee status and timing tips.
  • Twin-centre ideas and a booking checklist. Thailand + Vietnam, Sri Lanka + the Maldives, Kenya + Lamu, Japan + Thailand, and the steps to take whichever place wins.
  • Vietnam added as a full option. Visas (the same $25 e-visa for both passports), flights, costs, weather, a two-week Hanoi to Phu Quoc route, the Tet dates to avoid, official links, and a bar in the cost chart.
  • Site launched. Travel Plans home page with the first trip: Winter 2027, two weeks for a US and a Nigerian passport.
  • Winter 2027 comparison. Germany, Barbados and Japan compared on visas, flights, hotels, daily costs and weather, with a 14-night route for each.
  • Alternatives. Thailand, Kenya and Sri Lanka, plus Vietnam, Iceland, Ireland, China and Morocco checked, and the places ruled out.
  • Air Peace and connections. What the Lagos to Barbados airline is, and which connections to avoid on a Nigerian passport.
  • Cost chart. An animated chart of rough 14-night totals for the six main options, and official visa and tourism links for each.
